### 01 // Engineering & Craft

#### Software Test Engineering & Embedded Device Automation

Deeply involved in building and maintaining test automation frameworks for embedded devices and prototype hardware (specifically DAMF - Device Automation & Management Framework). Focusing on high reliability, reproducible runs, and eliminating flaky tests.

> **Environment:** Python, Linux daemons, serial communications, suckless toolchains, QEMU/virtualization.

#### Open Source Systems & Telemetry

* **[tcpflowy](https://pypi.org/project/tcpflowy/) (v1.0.0 on PyPI):** Fast terminal-based utility for capturing, filtering, and dissecting live TCP flows. Just published the official 1.0.0 release and made it available as a standard Python package (`pip install tcpflowy`).
* **[suckless-debian](https://github.com/diorgulescu/suckless-debian):** Evolved from the previous suckless-ubuntu setup into a streamlined, ultra-minimalist Debian environment powered by `dwm`, `dmenu`, and `st`. Now supporting `amd64`, `ARM`, and legacy `i386` architectures.
* **[DAMF](https://github.com/diorgulescu/DAMF):** Device Automation & Management Framework for embedded hardware testing and lab validation, actively receiving architecture and reliability updates.
* **[GirevoyPulse](https://github.com/diorgulescu/GirevoyPulse):** Native kettlebell telemetry and pacing app. Features native project setups for both **Xcode** (iOS/macOS) and **Android Studio**.

### 03 // Audio Explorations

#### Scentient Pencils & Analog Synthesis

Toying with hardware synthesizers, ambient texture generators, and field recordings. Recording unpolished living-room sessions that prioritize raw mood over studio perfection.

#### voidwave-lab // Open Source Audio Generators

Developing a series of open-source software tools for experimental music, glitch, and ambient under [voidwave-lab](https://github.com/voidwave-lab) on GitHub:

* **Modular Synthesizer:** Flexible patch-based software synth for generative sound design and complex modulation.
* **Korg MS-20 Mini Clone:** Software emulation capturing the aggressive dual resonant filters (HP/LP) and external patch bay.
* **Test Equipment Workbench:** Virtual studio environment based on 1950s/60s early electronic music lab instruments (signal generators, passive filters, and precision test equipment).
* **[Aetherium](https://github.com/voidwave-lab/Aetherium):** Unified multi-platform generative ambient soundscape and receptive music therapy sanctuary (Desktop, Web, iOS, Android). Procedural Web Audio DSP, orbital physics, and clinical ISO-principle vectoring.
