### 03 // Audio Explorations

#### Scentient Pencils & Analog Synthesis

Toying with hardware synthesizers, ambient texture generators, and field recordings. Recording unpolished living-room sessions that prioritize raw mood over studio perfection.

#### voidwave-lab // Open Source Audio Generators

Developing a series of open-source software tools for experimental music, glitch, and ambient under [voidwave-lab](https://github.com/voidwave-lab) on GitHub:

* **Modular Synthesizer:** Flexible patch-based software synth for generative sound design and complex modulation.
* **Korg MS-20 Mini Clone:** Software emulation capturing the aggressive dual resonant filters (HP/LP) and external patch bay.
* **Test Equipment Workbench:** Virtual studio environment based on 1950s/60s early electronic music lab instruments (signal generators, passive filters, and precision test equipment).
