STRONGFIRST CERTIFICATION PROTOCOL
FRAMEWORK // 02 • TARGET // STRONGFIRST GIRYA 1 & BODYWEIGHT • STRUCTURE // 3 PROGRESSIVE PHASES

SFG I & SFB Prep Blueprint

A progressive 8-month training program structured in 3 major phases to prepare for StrongFirst Girya (SFG) Level 1 and StrongFirst Bodyweight (SFB) certifications. Combines the Rite of Passage, alactic endurance (A+A), and a 3-consecutive-day certification stress simulation.

PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (ROP & S&S) • PHASE 2: DOUBLE KB & A+A EMOM • PHASE 3: CERTIFICATION SIMULATION
01
PHASE 01 // SFG LEVEL 1 + SFB

Building the Foundation (Rite of Passage & S&S)

LIFT HEAVY. BE KIND.
Fat Loss & CNS Management (+40): Moderate caloric deficit. To safeguard the central nervous system and promote lipid oxidation without cortisol spikes, main volume is loaded into 3 primary days (Mon-Wed-Fri), keeping Tuesday and Thursday as flexible modules dedicated to movement variety and active tissue recovery.
Monday: Heavy Day (RoP)
Maximal Volume & Tension
S&S Warm-up + Bent Press Prep
Prying Squats, Halos, Arm Bars
Including bent-arm bar variations for thoracic and shoulder unlocking.
Primary Block: Rite of Passage
Clean & Press + Tactical Pull-ups (SFB)
4–5 ladders of 1, 2, 3, 4 (up to 5 reps each, max 5x5)
Execution: 1 C&P left/right, 1 strict tactical pull-up, rest. Advance to the 2-rep rung, etc. 3–5 min rest between full ladders.
Ballistic Finisher
Heavy Two-Arm Swings
5–12 minutes (Sets of 10 reps on the minute / alactic)
FLEXIBLE MODULE: Tuesday (SFB Variety)
Slow-Motion Turkish Get-Up & SFB Progressions
3x1+1 ultra-slow TGUs. Assisted Pistols (3x5–8) + Elevated One-Arm Push-up progressions (3x5 line integrity focus).
Wednesday: Light Day (RoP)
Neural Regeneration & Rhythm
Primary Block: Regressed RoP
Clean & Press + Tactical Pull-ups
3 ladders of 1, 2, 3 reps
Keep the same working weight as Monday, but slash overall volume significantly.
SFG Technical Skill
Kettlebell Snatch Practice
5–10 sets × 10 reps (5L / 5R)
Crisp, submaximal groove work focusing on trajectory and bell float. Stay completely fresh.
FLEXIBLE MODULE: Thursday (Bent Press & S&S)
Bent Press Preliminaries & Simple & Sinister
High & Low Windmills (3x3). Follow with 100 One-Arm Swings (10x10) + 10 total alternating TGUs at conversation pace.
Wednesday and Thursday clear metabolic waste and accelerate central nervous recovery.
Friday: Medium Day (RoP)
Structural Volume Bridge
Primary Block: Medium RoP
Clean & Press + Tactical Pull-ups
5 ladders of 1, 2, 3 reps
The sweet spot of volume. Zero muscular failure, immaculate movement precision.
Ballistic Finisher
Heavy Two-Arm Swings
5–12 minutes (Sets of 10 reps)
WEEKEND: Aerobic Lipid Metabolism
LSD Cardio & Rucking
Saturday or Sunday: 45–60 minutes
Brisk outdoor rucking with weight vest in Zone 2 to stimulate fat oxidation and structural leg stamina.
Coach Directive: “Strength is a skill.” Never train to mechanical failure. In a caloric deficit, disciplined rest is just as anabolic as lifting.
02
PHASE 02 // SFG LEVEL 1 + SFB

Strength Acquisition & Alactic (A+A) Conditioning

LIFT HEAVY. BE KIND.
Fat Loss & CNS Management (+40): Working with double kettlebells drastically escalates metabolic output. We introduce the alactic EMOM snatch protocol (A+A) to incinerate body fat without creating destructive systemic acidosis.
Monday: Workout 1 (Double KB)
High-Density Double Complex
Double Kettlebell Circuit
3 Rounds • 5 min rest between rounds
Rest between exercises progressively drops over the weeks from 60s down to 20s.
Circuit Sequence
1. One-Arm Swing
10 Left / 10 Right
2. Double Clean & Press
5 reps
3. Double Clean & Front Squat
5 reps
4. Turkish Get-Up
1 Left / 1 Right
5. Kettlebell Snatch
10 Left / 10 Right
Wednesday: A+A Snatches
Anti-Glycolytic Endurance
Neural Preparation
Crisp Turkish Get-Ups
6 total reps (3/arm) as specific prep
Snatch A+A Protocol (EMOM)
Targeted Kettlebell Snatch
Duration: 15 minutes • EMOM
Perform 5 snatches right, then 5 left at the top of each minute. Rest the remainder of the minute.
FLEXIBLE MODULE: Tuesday (Justa Singles & SFB)
Bent Press (Justa Singles) & OAPU/Pistol
15–25 singles @ 70% 1RM on the minute. Easy Strength for OAPU & Pistols: 2 sets x 5 submaximal reps.
Thursday is a mandatory ACTIVE RECOVERY day: joint mobility and easy rucking.
Friday: Workout 2 (Double KB)
Structural Strength
Modified Double KB Circuit
3 Rounds • 5 min rest between rounds
Circuit 2 Sequence
1. Double Clean & Press
5 reps
2. Turkish Get-Up
1 Left / 1 Right
3. Double Clean & Front Squat
5 reps
4. One-Arm Swing
10 Left / 10 Right
5. Kettlebell Snatch
10 Left / 10 Right
SATURDAY: Heavy Engine & SFB
Heavy Swings EMOM & SFB Maintenance
10 sets x 10 reps EMOM (heaviest available bell). OAPU & Pistol: 2x5 easy, pristine technique.
Coach Directive: Wednesday’s A+A protocol builds mitochondrial volume. Keep heart rate under the anaerobic threshold—never let breathing devolve into desperate gasping.
03
PHASE 03 // SFG LEVEL 1 + SFB

SFG I Specificity

LIFT HEAVY. BE KIND.
Fat Loss & CNS Management (+40): Peak competition phase. The Friday-Saturday-Sunday block simulates the 3 consecutive days of testing stress encountered at certification. All circuits are performed with the official testing bell weight. SFB skills transition into “Maintenance/Easy” mode.
Certification Simulation (Days 1 & 2)
Consecutive Double KB Loading
Friday: SFG Day 1 (Workout 1)
Double KB Circuit (Official Test Weight)
3 Rounds • 5 min rest • Max 20s between movements
Swing (10L/10R) → Double C&P (5) → Double Squat (5) → TGU (1L/1R) → Snatch (10L/10R).
Saturday: SFG Day 2 (Workout 2)
Modified Double KB Circuit Under Cumulative Fatigue
3 Full Rounds • 5 min rest between rounds
Phase 2 Friday sequence. Laser focus on explosive hip drive despite systemic fatigue.
Prioritize complex carbohydrates and electrolytes throughout the weekend to refill muscle glycogen.
Sunday: Certification Simulation (Day 3)
Final Crucible & Mental Toughness
Sunday: SFG Day 3 (Workout 3)
Final Double KB Circuit
3 Full Rounds
Modified sequence testing core and grip integrity:
1. Double Clean & Front Squat
5 reps below parallel
2. One-Arm Swing
10 Left / 10 Right
3. Double Clean & Press
5 strict reps
4. Turkish Get-Up
1 Left / 1 Right
5. Kettlebell Snatch
10 Left / 10 Right
Monday is a mandatory ABSOLUTE REST day. Gentle walking only.
Tuesday: Snatch Test Day
Testing Benchmark Readiness
Official Snatch Test Practice
100 Snatches / Official Testing Weight / max 5 min
Pacing strategy: 20/20, 15/15, 10/10, 5/5. Repeat block 2–3 times over the cycle with 5–10 min full recovery between attempts.
SFB & Bent Press Neurological Maintenance
Pistol Squat, OAPU & Bent Press
Neural Pathway Maintenance
SFB: 2 sets x 3–5 reps (crisp and easy). Bent Press: 2–3 clean singles with moderate load (60–70%).
Thursday: Short Ballistic Finisher
Heavy Swings EMOM
10 minutes EMOM (10L / 10R) + easy rucking
Coach Directive: The Snatch Test is conquered with a cold, focused mind and biomechanical precision. Guard your hand calluses and stay relentlessly hydrated.
