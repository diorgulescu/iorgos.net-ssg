Tension Spectrum

FREQUENCY // 5-DAY HYBRID SPLIT • GOAL // HYPERTROPHY WITHOUT CNS EXHAUSTION • RECOVERY // HIGH COMPLIANCE

Tension Spectrum is an advanced training framework centered on muscular tension manipulation. Built on functional strength fundamentals from the StrongFirst school of strength, it utilizes movement complexes to simultaneously forge systemic resilience, extreme mobility, and functional hypertrophy without frying the central nervous system.

Weekly Schedule


MONDAY: STRENGTH & BALLISTICS

Focus: Push-Pull Synergy & Alactic Power

  • Strength Block (Maximal Tension): Tactical Pull-ups / Gymnastic Rings or Heavy Rows + Hardstyle / SFB Push-ups.
    • Format: 3–5 sets x 3–5 reps (focus on total tension, generous rest intervals).
  • Ballistic Block (Pure Alactic): Heavy Swings or Snatches.
    • Format: Short, explosive sets, maximal intent, full recovery between efforts.
  • Finisher: Loaded Carries (Farmer’s Walk or Suitcase Carry).

TUESDAY: LOADED STRETCHING & ZONE 2

Focus: Mobility Under Load & Aerobic Base

Complex 1: Structural Mobility

3–5 rounds / arm, light to medium kettlebell. A master test of thoracic mobility, hip opening, and shoulder girdle stability.

  1. Drop into Deep Squat (hold at the bottom in controlled isometric tension)
  2. Curl Clean (clean directly from bottom of the squat to rack)
  3. Sots Press (overhead press directly from squat → full overhead lockout)
  4. Stand Up (drive out of squat maintaining kettlebell locked out overhead)
  5. Windmill (hinge with arm locked, open the hip, tap floor)
  6. Lower to Rack Position
  7. Bent Press (secondary overhead press via torso displacement into asymmetric lockout)
  8. TGU Down (controlled descent to the floor, arm fully locked overhead)
  9. TGU Up (full Turkish Get-Up back to standing; close the circle)
  • Aerobic Conditioning: Zone 2 run or pure steady-state cardio for mitochondrial biogenesis.

WEDNESDAY: ACTIVE RECOVERY

Focus: Regeneration & Tissue Reset

  • Gentle movement and walking.
  • Fast & Loose drills (limb shaking to dissipate residual tension).
  • Decompression stretching and joint circles.

THURSDAY: STRENGTH-ENDURANCE

Focus: Glycolytic Capacity & Mental Fortitude (Lactic Release)

Complex 2: Descending Ladder Protocol (10 - 8 - 5 - 3 - 2 - 1)

Recommended weight: ~80% 1RM (a kettlebell you can press for roughly 12 strict reps all-out).

  • Ladder Execution (Left / Right): [X] Clean & Press + [X] Kettlebell Front Squats (in the rack position).
  • Iron Rule: The bell NEVER touches the floor during a ladder step! The transition from Press to Squat and the hand change (Swing switch) are continuous without ground rest. The final step (1) is completed as classic Iron Cardio (1 Clean + 1 Press + 1 Squat per arm).
  • Rest Interval: Active recovery 2–3+ minutes in Fast & Loose fashion. Do not start the next rung until heart rate has stabilized and nasal breathing is fully restored.

FRIDAY: NEURO-RECOVERY

Focus: Structural Unloading & Groove Reinforcement

  • Minimalist session (in the vein of Simple & Sinister) at moderate intensity (~70%).
  • Exclusive focus on crisp movement quality and flushing residual Thursday fatigue.
  • Weekend preparation.

SATURDAY & SUNDAY: TOTAL RECOVERY

Focus: Supercompensation

  • Rest, restorative sleep, and assimilation of structural adaptations.
