Training Frameworks

Telemetry: GirevoyPulse ↗

A structured collection of training programs, protocols, and conditioning blueprints conceived or adapted over years of kettlebell lifting, bodyweight mastery, and tendon rehabilitation. Shared freely for serious practitioners.

HYPERTROPHY & TENSION 01 // PROTOCOL

Tension Spectrum

An advanced training framework focused on muscle tension manipulation, specific kettlebell complexes (Complex 1: Structural Mobility, Complex 2: 10-8-5-3-2-1), loaded stretching, and supercompensation without central nervous system burnout.

CERTIFICATION // 8 MONTHS 02 // PROTOCOL

SFG I & SFB Prep

A progressive 3-phase blueprint designed for passing StrongFirst Girya Level 1 and StrongFirst Bodyweight certifications. Includes the Rite of Passage, alactic A+A EMOM endurance, Snatch Test conditioning, and weekend heavy double kettlebell simulations.

REHABILITATION & JIU-JITSU 03 // PROTOCOL

BJJ & Rehabilitation

Elbow recovery protocol (epicondylitis/tendonitis rehabilitation) combined with mitochondrial aerobic base building (Zone 2 via stationary bike, rucking, or slow running), ground mobility (Shrimpings, Bridge & Reach), and isometric grip strength.

CARDIOVASCULAR CONDITIONING 04 // PROTOCOL

Navy Seal Burpees & Zone 2

Bodyweight conditioning protocols executed at a steady, controlled pace for pure cardiovascular endurance without excessive systemic acidification. Designed for cardiac output and lipid metabolism maintenance.
