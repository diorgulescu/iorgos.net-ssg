BJJ Prep & Rehabilitation Protocol

A specialized protocol engineered for elbow tendonitis rehabilitation, building a solid aerobic base in Zone 2 (stationary bike, rucking, or nasal-breathing slow runs), mat-specific ground mobility for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and structural strength via Hardstyle kettlebells.

PERIOD // JULY – SEPTEMBER • DAILY 5–7 MIN ELBOW REHAB • ZONE 2 CONSTANT BASE
Daily Micro-Routine: Elbow & Forearm (5–7 minutes)
1. Wrist Isometric Hold (90°): 3 sets × 30–45 sec hold
2. Slow Eccentric Lowers: 3 sets × 10–12 reps (4–5 sec eccentric phase)
3. Rubber Band Finger Extensions: 2–3 sets × 15–20 reps
Weekly Training Blueprint 7-DAY PROTOCOL
Day Primary Focus Block 1: Strength / Core / Mobility Block 2: Aerobic Base (Zone 2)
MON Kettlebell & Aerobic Base
  • Goblet Squat (16 kg): 3 x 5 (3s pause at bottom)
  • Double KB Deadlift (2x20 kg): 4 x 8 (Dead-stop)
  • Turkish Get-Up (12–14 kg): 3 / side
 30 min (Continuous Zone 2) Options: Stationary bike, rucking, or nasal Zone 2 slow run
TUE BJJ Mobility & Core Mechanics
  • Bridge & Reach: 2 x 8 / side | Shrimpings: 3 x 10 / side
  • Hardstyle/RKC Push-ups: 3–4 x 3–5 reps on knuckles
  • Tuck Hollow Hold: 4 x 15–20 sec (lower back pinned to floor)
 Passive mobility: Couch Stretch & 90/90 (60s / side)
WED Cardio & Neuro-Recharge Complete rest for arms and upper body loading. 35–40 min (Easy pacing) Options: Stationary bike, rucking, or nasal Zone 2 slow run
THU Kettlebell & Upper Structure
  • Goblet Squat (16 kg): 3 x 5 | Single KB Deadlift (32 kg): 4 x 6–8
  • Turkish Get-Up (12–14 kg): 3–4 / side
  • Hardstyle Push-ups: 3–4 x 3–5 reps
  • Isometric Ring/Suspension Row Hold: 3 x 20–30 sec
 Optional: 15–20 min recovery Easy spinning, rucking, or light Zone 2 jog
FRI BJJ Mobility & Core Mechanics
  • Bridge & Reach + Shrimpings: 15 min continuous flow
  • Tuck Hollow Hold: 4 x 15–20 sec
  • Thoracic spine mobility & decompression: 5 min
 20–30 min (Very easy pacing) Options: Stationary bike, rucking, or nasal Zone 2 slow run
SAT Outdoor Aerobic Work & Stairs
  • Brisk walk on the park loop (1.6 km)
  • Stairs: 3–5 cadenced ascents (full-foot contact, drive through heels & glutes)
 Outdoor Aerobic Base: 45–60 min Rucking (weighted vest/backpack), brisk walk, or slow Zone 2 run
SUN Active Recovery & Family Time Casual family walk. No timer, no HR zones, zero structured stress. Total Restoration & Deep Sleep
Golden Rules for Structural Integrity & Elbow Health
HARDSTYLE PUSH-UPS:

100% tension, body rigid as a plank, pressurized exhalation (Power Breath). Maximum movement quality on 3–5 reps.

HOLLOW HOLD:

If your lumbar spine breaks contact with the floor by even 1 mm, terminate the set immediately. Shallow breathing "behind the shield".

ELBOW MONITORING:

If morning stiffness increases the next day, drop kettlebell volume by 20% and focus on eccentric wrist work.
