Kettlebell & Conditioning

Foundational principles of functional strength: full-body tension on presses and squats, pure alactic explosive power on ballistic swings, and instant Fast & Loose relaxation between sets.

1. Navy Seal Burpees: Aerobic Endurance Protocol

Navy Seal Burpees (the 6-count variation with 2 rigid push-ups and 3 alternating knee-to-chest tucks per rep) are one of the most effective muscular endurance tests without aggressive joint impact.

  • Recommended format: 10 sets of 5 repetitions (50 total reps), with rest intervals dictated strictly by nasal breathing recovery.
  • Technical standard: Every push-up is performed on knuckles or flat palms with a locked hollow-body trunk. Zero lumbar hyperextension.
  • Physiological objective: Stimulating fatty acid oxidation and mitochondrial capillary density.

2. Hardstyle Kettlebell Basics

The foundation of all structural athletic progress rests on mastering the core movements of the StrongFirst discipline:

  • The Swing (1-arm and 2-arm): Violent hip snap, ballistic trajectory, and standing plank lockout at the apex.
  • The Turkish Get-Up (TGU): Scapular stability, multi-planar hip mobility, and tri-planar movement coordination.
  • The Clean & Press: Strict overhead pressing strength anchored on gluteal tension and core bracing.

3. Cardiac Telemetry & Pacing: GirevoyPulse

For precise heart-rate telemetry and cadence tracking during ballistic kettlebell sets (EMOM, alactic conditioning circuits, and press ladders), I built the open source app GirevoyPulse, featuring native project targets for both Xcode (iOS/macOS) and Android Studio.
